Privacy Policy for DailyCut

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DailyCut ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. By using DailyCut you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

DailyCut does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email, or other directly identifying personal information. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information as described below.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as advertising identifiers), IP address, and information about how you interact with ads in order to serve and measure advertising. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and manage personalized ads in your device settings.

Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). We use Firebase to power the global leaderboard. When you submit a score, we store a randomly generated nickname, your score, and the level reached. This data is not linked to your real identity. See Firebase Privacy.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time, non-consumable "Remove Ads" purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information; all transactions are handled by the App Store.

4. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to display and measure advertising, operate the leaderboard, and improve the App. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyCut is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Choices

You can limit ad tracking through your device's privacy settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS). Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops the display of advertisements in the App.

7. Data Retention

Leaderboard entries are retained to operate the leaderboard. You may request deletion of a leaderboard entry by contacting us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
